Flagship project · GIP AI Innovation Challenge 2026

SHIELD — AI Agent for Logistics Dispute Resolution

“Smart Handler for Intelligent & Efficient Logistics Disputes.” Team Lead for an AI-enabled workflow designed to classify incoming complaints, detect customer tone, identify the right owner, and draft an SOP-compliant response.

🏆 2nd Place — GIP AI Innovation Challenge 2026

The problem

Customer service teams handled 300+ complaint tickets per week manually — reading, assessing severity, tracing SOPs, finding the right department, and drafting responses. Each ticket could take 30–60 minutes, while roughly 23% were misrouted or missed each week during peak periods.

My role

Led the project team through problem scoping, SOP and SLA logic definition, issue-category mapping, ownership rules, testing, and live demonstration using real ticket cases.

88% Faster processing
94% Classification accuracy
<30 min Average response time
<2% Missed / misrouted

Processing time dropped from 45 minutes to under 6 seconds per ticket; classification accuracy increased from a 77% manual baseline to 94%; average customer response time fell from approximately 4 hours to under 30 minutes; and the team estimated a 3x ROI within six months of rollout.
