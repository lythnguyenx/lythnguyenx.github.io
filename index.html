03 — Selected work
Flagship project · AI Innovation Challenge 2026

SHIELD — AI Agent for Logistics Dispute Resolution

"Smart Handler for Intelligent & Efficient Logistics Disputes." Team Lead for an AI agent that reads an incoming complaint and, in seconds, classifies severity, detects customer tone, identifies the right owner, and drafts an SOP-compliant response.

🏆 2nd Place — GIP AI Innovation Challenge 2026

The problem

My team handled 300+ complaint tickets a week entirely by hand — reading, judging severity, tracing SOPs, finding the right department, and drafting replies. Each ticket took 30–60 minutes, and roughly 23% were misrouted or missed each week, especially during peak hours.

My role

Led the team as we scoped the project, defined the SOP and SLA logic behind classification, mapped issue categories to the right PIC per department, and ran the live demo in front of judges using real ticket cases.

88%Faster processing
94%Classification accuracy
<30 minAvg. response time
<2%Tickets missed / misrouted

Processing time dropped from 45 minutes to under 6 seconds per ticket; classification accuracy rose from a 77% manual baseline to 94%; average customer response time fell from 4 hours to under 30 minutes; and the team estimated a 3x ROI within 6 months of rollout.
